## Final experience

Set goals and constraints → discover candidates → inspect business, valuation,
risk and events → receive an explained decision assessment → inspect allocation
or alternative → save the evidence and assumptions → revisit after refreshed data.
Existing holdings enter a hold/trim/sell review workflow. A stock outside the
top ten is not automatically a sell. Insufficient evidence is distinct from a
negative investment assessment. The product cannot promise complete information,
guaranteed profits or outperformance. Evidence gates remain separate from scores.

## 1. Complete stock evidence and refresh — partial, current priority

- Exact security/issuer/share-class evidence and supported dated universe.
- Broader usable financial inputs, reported shares and valuation evidence;
  explicitly label annual versus TTM and estimation assumptions.
- Independent financial, price, split and dividend-basis checks; missing and
  stale inputs cannot be silently substituted.
- Dependable bounded end-of-day refresh, retries, atomic publication,
  last-known-good recovery, failure messages and reproducible source dates/hashes.
- Resolve free-source feasibility and separate local-use/public-display rights.

Current: 88 issuer records, 89 price series including SPY, 501 listing/exchange
corroborations across 505 proxy securities, independent checks and operator
imports. Financial passes total 36; 21 fail and 31 lack required inputs. These
counts are not full-universe or valuation verification. Automatic daily refresh,
strict identity/index/valuation gates and broader coverage remain unfinished.

## 2. Finish strategy and user-context contracts — partial

- Keep the initial versioned quality/value strategy: ROA, operating margin,
  cash FCF margin, earnings yield and FCF yield; within-sector peer comparisons.
- Validate fiscal alignment, negative earnings, leverage, share classes,
  unsupported sectors, missing inputs and corporate actions. Financials and
  Real Estate remain outside the initial stock strategy's supported sectors.
- Record user-entered capital, recurring contribution intentions, horizon,
  risk/concentration limits, exclusions and existing holdings; no identity or
  brokerage connection required. Connect goals without inventing stock forecasts.
- Publish tested decision thresholds and how costs and available tax inputs
  affect an existing-holding review. Personal constraints do not rewrite the
  accounting-quality score.

Current: the five-feature experimental strategy, portfolio constraints,
exclusions, optional volatility ceiling, direct holdings exposure and supporting
goal tools work. A complete personalized decision contract and sell policy do not.

## 3. Build the complete stock decision engine — experimental selection exists

- Buy candidate: passes evidence, strategy and portfolio-fit rules; explain
  the case, weaknesses, alternatives and suggested allocation.
- Hold assessment: existing position continues to meet the documented thesis
  and portfolio conditions; explain what would require another review.
- Trim/sell review: separately test deterioration, valuation, concentration,
  changed user constraints and modeled costs/taxes. Do not trigger liquidation
  merely because the stock loses a ranking position.
- Pass: enough evidence exists but the candidate fails stated criteria; provide
  the reason. Insufficient evidence: withhold the investment assessment and
  identify the missing information instead of treating it as a financial failure.
- Versioned reasons and thresholds, separate evidence/quality/value/portfolio
  findings, and explicit conditions that would change the assessment.
- Connect accepted evidence to constrained stock selection, cent-reconciled
  amounts, recurring-cash allocation and cash left unassigned. Preserve original
  proposals separately from user changes. No executable orders.

Current: experimental ten-position construction and explanations work with
proxy/estimate inputs; verified recommendations are disabled. Complete hold,
trim/sell, pass-versus-insufficient-data decisions and user-goal integration are
new required work, not existing functionality.
